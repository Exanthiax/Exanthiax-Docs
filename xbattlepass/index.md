# xBattlepass
### **What is xBattlepass?**

xBattlepass is a fully customizable, feature-packed Battlepass plugin. It includes Daily, Weekly, and Monthly quests, along with infinite tiers and both Free and Premium rewards.

### **What makes xBattlepass different?**

xBattlepass, powered by libreforge, is the pinnacle of customization for Battlepass plugins. It offers much more than just distributing items, currency, XP, and permissions. With xBattlepass, players can receive powerful effects such as enchanting hand items, applying XP boosts, granting potion effects, filling hunger bars, doubling health, and more.

Take full control of the player experience with fully customizable GUIs, endless rewards, infinite tiers, and seamless integration with external plugins.
